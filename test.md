### Why This Structure Works:

1.  **Respects Both Audiences:** It doesn't remove critical technical details for developers; it just makes them opt-in. Non-technical readers are no longer forced to hit a wall.
2.  **Uses Effective Signposting:** The "Choose Your Path" section acts as a map. It acknowledges the different backgrounds of visitors and gives them agency, improving their experience.
3.  **Leads with Strength:** It keeps your most engaging content (the problem and the grand vision) at the top, which works for everyone.
4.  **Preserves All Content:** You don't have to change a single word of your carefully crafted text. You're just reordering it and adding a simple navigational layer.
5.  **Feels Modern:** This "choose your own adventure" style is common and user-friendly on modern websites, especially for technical products.

This approach allows you to keep all your excellent text while ensuring the page is navigable and valuable for every single visitor, regardless of their technical expertise.
